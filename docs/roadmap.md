# Product Roadmap

## 1. Status Legend
- ✅ **Implemented & Production Ready**: Fully functioning in current codebase.
- 🟡 **In Development / Beta**: Functional with mock or simulated backends.
- 📋 **Planned Features**: Future release targets.

---

## 2. Feature Progression

### 2.1 Core Platform & Consensus Engine
- ✅ **Jackpot Consensus Engine**: Real-time aggregation for SportPesa Mega, Betika Midweek, MozzartBet, Odibets.
- ✅ **Consensus Slip Auto-Generators**: Pure Consensus, Balanced Smart, and Value Risk slips.
- ✅ **Free Match Prediction Rooms**: Open discussion rooms for daily European & African matches.
- ✅ **PayGate Room Access**: KSh 10 flat unlock fee via M-Pesa or rewarded video ad watch.

### 2.2 AI Integrations
- ✅ **Ava 24/7 AI Customer Support**: Google Gemini 3.6 Flash assistant with Kenyan support persona.
- ✅ **Mshindi AI Sports Advisor**: Match stats, head-to-head analysis, and daily trivia generator.
- ✅ **Jackpot Webpage Parser API**: Admin tool to parse raw copied text into structured leg fixtures.

### 2.3 Community & Ratings
- ✅ **5-Star Punter Rating Engine**: Dynamic 1.0–5.0 star scoring based on settled jackpot accuracy.
- ✅ **Gameweek Leaderboard**: Weekly Mshindi Crown ranking with tie-breaking rules.
- ✅ **Social Bet Slip Sharing**: Share booking codes, like slips, and filter by tags.
- ✅ **Automated Content Moderation**: Filter for swear words, phone numbers, and external spam links.

### 2.4 Wallet & Payments
- ✅ **In-App Wallet**: Transaction ledgers, deposit/withdrawal logs, and balance tracking.
- 🟡 **M-Pesa Express (STK Push)**: Realistic PIN modal simulation (Ready for Daraja 2.0 API credentials).
- 📋 **Automated Daraja 2.0 Webhooks**: Instant B2C automated withdrawals to Safaricom accounts.

### 2.5 PWA & Mobile
- ✅ **Progressive Web App**: Service worker offline caching, manifest, and iOS/Android home screen install prompt.
- 📋 **Native Mobile Apps**: React Native / Flutter wraps for Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
